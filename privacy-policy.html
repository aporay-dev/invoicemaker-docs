Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [Nov 3 2025]

Last Updated: [Nov 3 2025]

InvoiceMaker ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using InvoiceMaker, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Quick Summary:

  • Your invoice data is stored locally on your device only - we do NOT have access to your invoices, clients, or business information
  • We use Google AdMob to show ads to free users (you can opt-out or upgrade to Pro)
  • Subscription payments are processed by Apple - we never see your payment details
  • You control your data and can delete it anytime from your device

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Data You Provide

When you use InvoiceMaker, you create and store information locally on your device, including:

  • Business Information: Your business name, address, contact details, and logo
  • Client Information: Names, companies, email addresses, and other contact details of your clients
  • Invoice Data: Invoice numbers, dates, items, prices, tax information, and payment records
  • Estimates: Estimate information including client details and pricing

Important: All of this data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not have servers, and we do not collect, access, or store this information on our end.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV)
  • Usage Data: App launch frequency, feature usage, screen views, and session duration
  • Crash Reports: Technical data about app crashes to improve stability

1.3 Third-Party Data Collection

We use third-party services that may collect information about you:

Google AdMob (Advertising)

For users on the free tier, we display ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID/IDFA)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)
  • App usage patterns

Note: Pro subscribers do not see ads, and AdMob does not track them.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

For subscription management, we use Apple's StoreKit framework, which processes:

  • Apple ID (encrypted)
  • Purchase history
  • Subscription status

Note: Apple handles all payment processing. We never see your credit card or payment information.

Learn more: Apple Privacy Policy

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect (primarily through third-party services) for the following purposes:

2.1 App Functionality

  • To provide invoice creation, management, and tracking features
  • To generate PDF invoices and estimates
  • To manage your subscription status (free vs. Pro)
  • To sync subscription status across your devices (via Apple)

2.2 Monetization (Free Tier Only)

  • To display relevant ads to free users via Google AdMob
  • To personalize ads based on your interests (with your consent via ATT)
  • To measure ad performance and improve ad relevance

2.3 App Improvement

  • To analyze app usage and improve features
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues
  • To understand which features are most valuable

2.4 Communication

  • To respond to support requests
  • To send important updates about the app (via App Store)
  • To request app reviews (via Apple's StoreKit Review API)

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your invoice data, client information, and business details are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is:

  • Never uploaded to our servers (we don't have servers)
  • Encrypted at rest using iOS device encryption
  • Protected by your device passcode/Face ID/Touch ID
  • Backed up to iCloud if you have iCloud Backup enabled (controlled by you)

3.2 Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Local data encryption via iOS
  • Secure communication with Apple's servers (HTTPS/TLS)
  • No plain-text storage of sensitive data
  • Regular security audits of third-party SDKs (AdMob, StoreKit)

Important Security Note: Since all your data is stored locally on your device, protecting your device is crucial. We recommend:

  • Using a strong device passcode
  • Enabling Face ID or Touch ID
  • Keeping your iOS updated
  • Not jailbreaking your device

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework (iOS 14.5+), we request your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes.

4.1 What Tracking Means

When you see the ATT prompt, we're asking permission to:

  • Access your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
  • Allow AdMob to show personalized ads based on your interests
  • Track ad clicks and conversions

4.2 Your Choices

  • Allow Tracking: You'll see more relevant ads, and it helps support the free version of the app
  • Ask App Not to Track: You'll still see ads, but they'll be generic (not personalized)
  • Upgrade to Pro: Remove all ads permanently

4.3 Changing Your Mind

You can change your tracking preference anytime:

  1. Go to iPhone Settings
  2. Scroll to Privacy & Security → Tracking
  3. Find InvoiceMaker and toggle tracking on/off

5.1 What We Do NOT Share

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Specifically:

  • Your invoice data stays on your device - we never see it
  • We don't share your business information with anyone
  • We don't share your client list with anyone

5.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We share limited data with trusted third parties only to provide app functionality:

Google AdMob (Free Users Only)

Apple Inc.

  • What they collect: Subscription status, purchase history

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court order, subpoena). However, since we don't have access to your invoice data, we cannot disclose what we don't have.

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is on your device - view it anytime in the app
  • Export: Export invoices as PDFs and share them
  • Modify: Edit any invoice, client, or business information
  • Delete: Delete individual items or all data from Settings

6.2 Complete Data Deletion

To permanently delete all your InvoiceMaker data:

  1. Delete the app from your device
  2. All local data is immediately deleted (invoices, clients, settings)

6.3 Subscription Management

To manage or cancel your Pro subscription:

  1. Go to iPhone Settings
  2. Tap [User Name] → Subscriptions
  3. Select InvoiceMaker
  4. Cancel or modify your subscription

If you're a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect

  • Opt-out of the sale of your information (we don't sell your data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

For EU/UK Residents (GDPR)

If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority
